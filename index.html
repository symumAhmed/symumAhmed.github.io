01 / About

Learning, researching, and building with purpose.

I’m interested in the space where rigorous thinking meets practical implementation.

My academic background spans Computer Science & Engineering and Applied Statistics & Data Science. I use this combination to approach research problems through careful data preparation, statistical reasoning, model development and evaluation.

My research experience includes medical image analysis, deep learning and healthcare data analytics. I have also worked on time-series forecasting of acute watery diarrhoea caseloads, comparing statistical and machine-learning forecasting approaches to support hospital planning.
